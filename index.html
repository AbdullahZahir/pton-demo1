Welcome to the kitchen

You do not need to know how to bake.

Really. Some of us grew up with a rolling pin in hand, and some of us learned what "fold, don't stir" means about ten minutes before our first meeting. Both kinds of people leave with something warm in their hands.

Baking Bad is a place to make things with your hands after a week of making things with your head. Come alone, come with your roommate, come because you smelled something good in the hallway.

Bring: yourself. Leave with: dessert, flour on your sleeve, and a few new names you actually remember.

The method

Joining, in three steps

How to end up with an apron

  1. Say hi. Reach out through the contacts on our More Info page and we'll add you to the list.
  2. Show up to a bake night. No sign-up fee, no audition, no tragic backstory required.
  3. Claim a station. Whisking, rolling, decorating, or professional taste-testing — all legitimate roles.
